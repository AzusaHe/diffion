NP Inverse Benchmark

Recovering D(c) and t₊(c) from constant-current concentration data c(x,t). v(x,t) is reconstructed from c by volume conservation, and c, v feed two families of methods: learned MLP/FNO baselines and fitting methods. All results are scored in one place.

Pre-process

v is reconstructed

In 1-D, volume conservation ties v(x,t) to c(x,t), so v is computed from c by integrating ∂c/∂t over x. Nothing is trained for it.

Learned baselines

Two training routes

① Direct prediction: trained on synthetic pairs against the true D(c), t₊(c), not the c-fit data loss of a PINN. ② Test-time training (TTT): starts from the supervised net and takes 150 AdamW steps per material on the PDE residual of the input c, v, with no labels. It is not a PINN either.

Fitting methods

Two PINN builds PARTIAL

c_net + v_net learns v as its own network, so no integration is needed. c_net only gets v by integrating ∂c/∂t over x at each time. Both now run with one total loss and one Adam (no staging); with that setup neither recovers θ, so staged training comes next.
